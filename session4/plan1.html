Session 4 - Sustainable and Eco-tourism

GSEA Semester 1 Lesson Outline

Session 4 Lesson Plan (2 Hours)

A 2-hour interactive session comparing genuine ecotourism with greenwashing, practicing mixed conditionals and connectors, solving TOEIC reading questions, and designing a sustainable eco-resort.

0-15
1.

Part 1: Warm-Up Discussion — Real Eco-Tourism vs. Greenwashing (15 mins)

Examine three real-world travel projects (in Costa Rica, Kenya, and Norway) to see how much money stays in the local community and the real environmental benefits created, compared to superficial marketing.

15-35
2.

Part 2: Grammar in Context — Mixed Conditionals & Connecting Words (20 mins)

Contrasting past conservation decisions with present outcomes (If + had + V3, would + V1), present status with past outcomes (If + were, would have + V3), and formal connecting words for goals and rules (so as to, in order that, provided that, on condition that).

35-55
3.

Part 3: TOEIC Practice & Error Logging — Parts 5 & 6 (20 mins)

Timed reading set on sustainable tourism certification notices; practicing conditional sentences, connecting words, and logging errors.

55-85
4.

Part 4: Collaborative Project Workshop — The Eco-Resort Blueprint (30 mins)

Internet-enabled team challenge with a €2,500,000 green endowment. Teams choose a fragile ecosystem (temperate fjord, Mediterranean wetland, or alpine valley) and design a low-impact, community-owned resort integrating target grammar.

85-100
5.

Part 5: Project Pitches & Stakeholder Q&A — The Green Investor Forum (15 mins)

2-minute pitches where peer groups act as the Sustainability Investment Board, asking teams about visitor limits, clean energy, local community benefits, and connecting words.

100-110
6.

Part 6: Vocabulary Speaking Game — Taboo (10 mins)

Fast-paced 45-second fluency game reinforcing critical sustainable tourism vocabulary: Greenwashing, Biodiversity Corridor, Community-Based Tourism, Carbon Offset, and Rewilding.

110-120
7.

Part 7: Class Wrap-Up & FE Check (10 mins)

Continuous assessment log, reviewing genuine ecotourism vs. greenwashing, applying grammar to the FE portfolio, and final preparation for Session 5.
